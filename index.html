How it works

  • It adapts. Every topic has 6 levels. A few right in a row moves your child up; a wrong streak eases it back — so practice always sits at the right challenge.
  • It can't be gamed. Numbers and content re-randomise every attempt, so children learn the method instead of memorising answers.
  • It teaches on mistakes. A wrong answer shows why that choice was tempting, the worked solution, and the key idea — then re-tests it later (spaced review).
  • You can see progress. A Parent page shows an explainable readiness band per subject, tuned to your target board (GL / CEM / ISEB / CSSE).
